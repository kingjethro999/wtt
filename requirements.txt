The exact filenames for Amazon's TrueType fonts vary by weight and style. Amazon uses AmazonEmber_Rg.ttf for its primary brand font and Bookerly-Regular.ttf for its reading serif font. [1, 2] 
The exact .ttf filename extensions across their complete font packages map to these primary weights:
Amazon Ember (UI & Brand Font)

AmazonEmber_Rg.ttf (Regular)
AmazonEmber_RgIt.ttf (Regular Italic)
AmazonEmber_Bd.ttf (Bold)
AmazonEmber_Lt.ttf (Light)
AmazonEmber_Th.ttf (Thin) [1] 

Bookerly (Kindle & Reading Font)

Bookerly-Regular.ttf (Regular)
Bookerly-Bold.ttf (Bold)
Bookerly-Italic.ttf (Italic)
Bookerly-BoldItalic.ttf (Bold Italic) [2] 

How to Access Them legally
Both fonts are proprietary and owned by Amazon (designed by Dalton Maag). [3] 

You can download the official developers' asset package directly via the Amazon Developer Portal.
Alternatively, developers frequently pull open-source mirrors of the compiled .ttf files directly from community repos like the jsDelivr CDN Mirror or public GitHub Font Trees. [1, 4, 5]
